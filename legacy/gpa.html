Admission to the M.Sc. Informatics at TUM runs through a two-stage Eignungsverfahren: your bachelor grade and your written documents are scored in a point system, and depending on the score you are admitted directly, rejected, or invited to an interview. There is no single published cut-off grade, so set the goal above yourself and check the current program page before each application round.

The exact TU Darmstadt formula this app uses

§ 28 (1)(2) APB — the Gesamtnote is computed from the module grades. The Ordnung des Studiengangs Informatik (B.Sc.) 2023 says under zu § 28 (2): Soweit in Anhang I nicht anders festgelegt, gehen die Modulnoten entsprechend der in den Modulen erworbenen Leistungspunkte in die Gesamtnote ein. Anhang I (the Studien- und Prüfungsplan) leaves the column Gewichtung f. Gesamtnote empty for every module — so every module is weighted by its CP, including the 12 CP Bachelorarbeit. No module gets a bonus factor.

Gesamtnote = Σ (CP_i × Note_i) / Σ CP_i over all counted, graded modules then: keep the first 2 decimals, truncate the rest (no rounding) — § 28 (2)

§ 25 (1) APB — modules graded bestanden / nicht bestanden are not part of the calculation. That is why your 1 CP Erfolgreich ins Informatik-Studium starten never touches the GPA.

§ 25 (5) APB — in areas where you choose between modules, grades count beginnend mit der besten Leistung, bis zur vorgeschriebenen Anzahl der Leistungspunkte. Best grades first; the excess portion (der überschießende Anteil) is dropped, partial CP included. Your Leistungsspiegel prints the caps for each area:

  • Wahlpflichtbereich — min 10 CP, max 35 CP count for the grade
  • Informatik-Wahlbereiche — min 5 CP, max 30 CP
  • Fachprüfungen (both together) — min 30 CP, max 40 CP
  • Studienbegleitende Leistungen — min 9 CP, max 18 CP
  • Informatik (B1, everything above) — min 48 CP, max 49 CP
  • Studium Generale — min 5 CP, max 6 CP
  • Pflichtbereich 114 CP and Bachelorarbeit 12 CP — no cap, everything counts

The caps are applied bottom-up: first inside each leaf area, then again at the parent. This app does exactly that. Modules dropped by a cap are shown greyed out.

§ 28 (3) APB — the final word on the Zeugnis: 1,0–1,59 sehr gut · 1,6–2,59 gut · 2,6–3,59 befriedigend · 3,6–4,09 ausreichend · from 4,1 nicht ausreichend.

Zusätzliche Leistungen (your Fachsprachekurs Mathematik, 1,7) are extra achievements outside the degree structure and do not enter the Gesamtnote — the Leistungsspiegel already shows them with no Angerechnet value.

Does a 5.0 hurt my GPA? — short answer: no

No. A failed exam never enters the Gesamtnote. Only the grade of the attempt you eventually pass goes into the module grade (APB § 25 (3)), and the Gesamtnote is built from module grades (§ 28 (1)). A module you have not passed has no module grade at all — in your Leistungsspiegel those show as unvollständig, not as 5,0.

What a 5.0 actually costs you:

  • Attempts. § 30 (1): a Fachprüfung may be repeated twice — 3 attempts in total. Fail the second repeat and you can apply once per degree for a mündliche Ergänzungsprüfung (§ 32), within 4 weeks of the result. Miss that deadline and the exam is endgültig nicht bestanden.
  • Time. A retake usually costs a semester, which delays the whole plan.
  • Nothing else. No grade penalty, no record on the Zeugnis.

The dangerous rule is the opposite one. § 30 (1): Bestandene Prüfungen können nicht wiederholt werden. A voluntary retake to improve a grade (§ 30 Abs. 1a) only exists for exams marked with an x in the Studien- und Prüfungsplan — and the B.Sc. Informatik 2023 plan marks none. So every grade you already have is permanent. A 4,0 you scraped through in the first year can never be repaired; only the modules still open can move your average.

Where a bad grade can still be deleted: the elective areas are marked Typ § 30 Abs. 5 (Wahlpflichtbereich, Informatik-Wahlbereiche — one deselection allowed) and Typ § 30 Abs. 6 (Studienbegleitende Leistungen, Studium Generale — unlimited deselection). A module there that you have not yet completed can be abgewählt on request: the required retakes disappear, failed attempts are not counted, and the module never appears on the Zeugnis. So if an elective is going badly and you have not finished it yet, swapping it for another one is free. Pflichtmodule cannot be dropped (§ 30 (3)).

Why TUCaN's GPA field does not match this app

Mid-degree, the Gesamt-GPA printed at the bottom of your Leistungsspiegel will usually differ from the number here — often by several hundredths. Neither side is wrong; they are two different quantities.

TUCaN's field is a forecast. TU Darmstadt says so directly: TUCaN errechnet den vorläufigen GPA von offenen Modulen und Prüfungsordnungsbereichen über Schätzungen, and that this preliminary value besser oder schlechter als der tatsächliche Wert ausfallen kann. Areas you have not started (Studienbegleitende Leistungen, Studium Generale, Bachelorarbeit, the missing Fachprüfungen CP) are filled with estimates, and those estimates move the number. TUCaN also prints 3 decimals where § 28 (2) truncates to 2 — a further sign it is not the § 28 result.

This app computes the § 28 result over the grades you actually have, with no estimates: the CP-weighted mean, capped best-first per area, truncated to two decimals. Work it through by hand on your own transcript and you will land on the same value.

Σ (CP × Note) over every counted, graded module ───────────────────────────────────────────── → truncate to 2 decimals Σ CP of those modules

Which one to plan against? This one. It is what gets printed on the Zeugnis and what an application is judged on. Treat TUCaN's field as a rough live indicator that converges to this value as areas close.
